What we do

Panel processing from raw board to packed carton

We turn particleboard and MDF into finished cabinets, desks and storage. Every step below runs in our Hai Phong factory, so tolerances and finish stay under one roof.

01 · BOARD

Board intake

Particleboard and MDF bought from qualified mills, checked and logged per order.

9 mm, 16 mm, 18 mm stock
2440 × 1220 mm sheets
02 · CUT

Panel saw & CNC nesting

Straight parts are cut in stacks on computer panel saws; shaped parts and cut-outs go to CNC nesting.

Nanxing NPC330S panel saws
Nanxing CNC nesting centres
03 · BORE

CNC drilling

Cam, dowel and shelf-pin holes drilled in one pass from the drawing.

Nanxing CNC boring
Nanxing CNC PTP centre
04 · EDGE

Edge banding & grooving

Straight edges run through automatic and double-end edge banders; curved edges are wrapped on profile machines.

PVC band, colour-matched
05 · PACK

RTA packing

Panels, hardware kit and instructions boxed per set, with EPS protection.

Customer cartons & labels
Palletised for containers

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Where is Huan Mao International's factory?

Our factory is in Ke Sat Commune, Hai Phong City, northern Vietnam, close to the port of Hai Phong.

What does Huan Mao International manufacture?

Wood panel furniture from melamine-faced particleboard and MDF: classroom cubby storage, mobile storage carts, coat lockers, book displays, storage cabinets, office credenzas, consoles, sliding-door cabinets and standing-desk tops, packed flat with hardware kits.

Do you offer OEM and ODM?

Yes. Most of our work is OEM, built to the customer's drawings and bill of materials. We also propose our own designs (ODM) for customers who want a ready-developed product range.

Which markets do you supply?

Our main market is the United States. Products are packed flat as ready-to-assemble kits for container shipping.

What equipment does Huan Mao International use?

Most of our line is Nanxing Machinery CNC equipment: NPC330S computer panel saws with automatic loaders, CNC nesting centres with automatic loading and unloading, a CNC point-to-point machining centre and CNC boring machines. Edges are finished on automatic and double-end edge banders and profile edge wrapping machines, and we also run a glue spreader with a 60-ton hydraulic cold press.

Is Huan Mao International certified?

Yes. Huan Mao International holds ISO 9001 (quality management) and ISO 14001 (environmental management) certification. FSC-certified board is available on request through our FSC-certified suppliers.

Does your board meet U.S. formaldehyde rules?

Yes. We use board from suppliers certified to CARB Phase 2 / TSCA Title VI. The formaldehyde certificates are issued to our board suppliers, and we can provide copies on request.

Who are your customers?

We supply established U.S. furniture brands in the education and office furniture markets. Customer names are kept confidential. Annual sales are around US$10 million.

Who manages the factory?

Huan Mao International was established in Vietnam in 2024 under Taiwanese management and employs about 100 people.

What is your minimum order and lead time?

Minimum order quantity and lead time depend on the product, materials and order size. Send us your drawings or product list and we will quote the MOQ and lead time together with the price.
