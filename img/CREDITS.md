# Nanxing Machinery product photos

Files named `nx-*.jpg` are product photos/renders from Nanxing Machinery (南興裝備), taken from the
equipment parameter list and catalogue the owner supplied. The owner confirmed on 2026-11-08 that Nanxing
gave permission to use them. They show the machine models listed in the owner's equipment list, but are
manufacturer images, not photos taken in the Huan Mao factory.
